# Output Specification

Title: **[Acquirer]–[Target]: Acquisition Analysis Report**

Display the research cut-off date, valuation date, currency, transaction status, and evidence scope below the title.

Use the headings below. If evidence does not support an analysis, retain the heading and briefly state the limitation and its consequences.

## 1. Executive Summary

- **1.1 Overall Assessment:** Whether consideration is supportable and what the acquirer should do.
- **1.2 Key Results:** Valuation, synergies, returns, and principal drivers.
- **1.3 Key Risks and Conditions:** Major risks, open issues, and recommendation conditions.

## 2. Transaction Overview

- **2.1 Terms and Timeline:** Parties, perimeter, status, timeline, and concise terms table.
- **2.2 Strategic Rationale:** Objectives, target contributions, and need for ownership.
- **2.3 Alternatives:** Acquisition compared with feasible alternatives.

## 3. Target Assessment and Due Diligence

- **3.1 Business and Competition:** Business quality, market position, and dependencies.
- **3.2 Financial Review:** Financial quality, cash needs, and forecast credibility.
- **3.3 Assets and Liabilities:** Assets, obligations, contracts, and exposures.
- **3.4 People and Operations:** Management, retention, scalability, and integration readiness.
- **3.5 Key Findings:** Findings, evidence, implications, and follow-up diligence.

## 4. Valuation Analysis

- **4.1 Operating Assumptions:** Standalone case and business drivers.
- **4.2 Valuation Methods:** Methods, inputs, adjustments, and limitations.
- **4.3 Valuation Results:** Methods and cases compared, with uncertainty explained.
- **4.4 Equity Value Bridge:** Enterprise value, claims, and relevant existing ownership.
- **4.5 Offer Comparison:** Actual or proposed consideration and performance needed to support it.

## 5. Synergies and Value Creation

- **5.1 Revenue and Cost Synergies:** Benefits and why ownership enables them.
- **5.2 Integration Costs and Timing:** Costs, dependencies, responsibilities, and timing.
- **5.3 Synergy Risks:** Delivery risks and adverse effects.
- **5.4 Value Creation Bridge:** Standalone equity, net synergies, fees, integration, price, and retained value; benefits needed to justify the premium.

## 6. Deal Structure, Financing and Returns

- **6.1 Payment Terms:** Consideration mechanics and risk allocation.
- **6.2 Financing and Liquidity:** Sources and uses, funding capacity, covenants, and capital-allocation effects.
- **6.3 Dilution and Leverage:** Issuance, ownership, price exposure, and leverage.
- **6.4 Acquisition Returns:** Defined measures, baseline, assumptions, and drivers.
- **6.5 Tax and Accounting:** Material effects and specialist-review needs.

## 7. Deal Risks and Scenarios

- **7.1 Closing Risks:** Completion risks, mitigants, and remaining exposure.
- **7.2 Operating and Integration Risks:** Risks to performance and synergy delivery.
- **7.3 Scenarios and Sensitivities:** Cases, decision-critical variables, and break-even thresholds.
- **7.4 Execution Plan:** Actions, owners, dependencies, milestones, and performance measures.

## 8. Offer Strategy and Recommendation

- **8.1 Price and Terms:** Supported position on price, structure, and retained value.
- **8.2 Negotiation Priorities:** Protections, concessions, and acceptable trade-offs.
- **8.3 Recommendation and Next Steps:** Action, conditions, outstanding work, owners, and change triggers.

## 9. References

- **9.1 Sources:** Dates, links or document locations, conflicts, and exclusions.
- **9.2 Assumptions and Calculations:** Inputs, formulas, schedules, and model locations. Keep essential assumptions beside results.
- **9.3 Open Issues:** Decision-critical gaps, consequences, and evidence needed.
