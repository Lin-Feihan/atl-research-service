# Acquisition Analysis (Acquirer Side) — Question List

These questions collect the acquirer's mandate and available information before research. Users may answer "Not specified," "Unknown," or "Not available." Missing answers must not be converted into invented facts, preferences, or requirements.

| Question | Field ID | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| What is the deal background? | `deal_background` | Describe the opportunity, transaction history, current status, and decision to be supported. |
| What are BuyCo's objectives? | `acquisition_objectives` | Describe the strategic, operating, or financial objectives and why acquisition is being considered. |
| Are any price or terms proposed? | `proposed_price_and_terms` | Provide any proposed consideration, value definition, payment mechanics, perimeter, and material terms. State if no offer exists. |
| What are the funding and return requirements? | `funding_and_return_requirements` | Provide any financing constraints, liquidity needs, capital-allocation priorities, or defined return requirements. |
| What financials and forecasts are available? | `available_financials_and_forecasts` | Identify available statements, forecasts, assumptions, periods, and their sources. Distinguish management forecasts from analyst scenarios. |
| What are the integration priorities? | `integration_priorities` | Describe integration objectives, priorities, constraints, retention concerns, and known dependencies. |
| What approvals and deadlines apply? | `approvals_and_deadlines` | Identify known approvals, decision dates, contractual deadlines, and timing constraints. |
| What are the negotiation priorities? | `negotiation_priorities` | Identify price, payment, protections, control, timing, or other priorities and any explicit limits. |
| What documents are available, and what's in scope? | `available_documents_and_scope` | List available documents and locations, access restrictions, included work, and exclusions. Listing a document does not establish that the runtime can access it. |
