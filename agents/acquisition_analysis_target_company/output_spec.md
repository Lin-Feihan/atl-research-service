# Output Specification

Title: **[Target]: Assessment of the Acquisition Offer from [Buyer]**

Display the research cut-off date, valuation date, currency, transaction status, and evidence scope below the title.

Use the headings below. If evidence does not support an analysis, retain the heading and briefly state the limitation and its consequences.

## 1. Executive Summary

- **1.1 Overall Assessment:** Offer assessment and recommended action.
- **1.2 Value Comparison:** Value relative to independence and credible alternatives, including realizable proceeds.
- **1.3 Key Risks and Conditions:** Payment, liquidity, and closing risks, gaps, and recommendation conditions.

## 2. Offer Overview

- **2.1 Background and Timeline:** Parties, perimeter, history, timeline, and status.
- **2.2 Offer Terms:** Consideration, payment mechanics, adjustments, timing, and conditions.
- **2.3 Shareholder Interests:** Evidenced priorities, holder differences, and management conflicts.

## 3. Due Diligence and Standalone Valuation

- **3.1 Business and Financial Review:** Business quality, performance, cash needs, and forecasts.
- **3.2 Diligence Findings:** Material findings and effects on value, proceeds, and closing.
- **3.3 Valuation Methods and Assumptions:** Drivers, methods, inputs, comparables, and limitations.
- **3.4 Valuation Results:** Methods and cases compared; supported values distinguished from illustrative scenarios.
- **3.5 Equity Value and Allocation:** Enterprise value, claims, security rights, and allocation limits.
- **3.6 Funding and Dilution:** Financing needs, feasibility, terms, and existing-holder dilution.

## 4. Offer Assessment

- **4.1 Offer Value and Premium:** Consideration and standalone value at consistent dates, definitions, and supported benchmarks.
- **4.2 Stock and Contingent Payments:** Buyer equity quality, payment mechanics, restrictions, and realization risk.
- **4.3 Shareholder Proceeds and Liquidity:** Allocations, deductions, receipt and sale timing, and tax limits.

## 5. Buyer Rationale and Deal Certainty

- **5.1 Buyer Strategy and Synergies:** Objectives, benefits, costs, timing, and evidence.
- **5.2 Buyer Alternatives:** Alternatives, scarcity, and bargaining implications.
- **5.3 Financial Capacity:** Ability to fund consideration and perform obligations.
- **5.4 Approvals and Closing Conditions:** Requirements, status, and unresolved matters.
- **5.5 Termination and Failure:** Rights, remedies, risk allocation, and delay/failure effects.

## 6. Strategic Alternatives

- **6.1 Comparison of Alternatives:** Offer, independence, and other options compared by value, funding, dilution, liquidity, timing, risk, evidence, and feasibility.
- **6.2 Preferred Alternative:** Most credible option, executability, and conditions under which it becomes preferable.

## 7. Risks and Scenario Analysis

- **7.1 Principal Risks:** Consequences, mitigants, remaining exposure, and interactions.
- **7.2 Scenario Analysis:** Conditional outcomes and proceeds at consistent dates.
- **7.3 Key Sensitivities:** Decision-critical variables and thresholds changing the offer's attractiveness.

## 8. Negotiation Strategy and Recommendation

- **8.1 Value and Terms:** Supported value position, range, conditions, and trade-offs.
- **8.2 Negotiation Priorities:** Protections, concessions, evidence, and holder conflicts within contractual limits.
- **8.3 Recommendation and Next Steps:** Action, conditions, diligence, owners, decisions, and change triggers.

## 9. References

- **9.1 Sources:** Dates, links or document locations, conflicts, and exclusions.
- **9.2 Assumptions and Calculations:** Inputs, formulas, schedules, and model locations supporting valuation, funding, dilution, and proceeds.
- **9.3 Open Issues:** Decision-critical gaps, consequences, and evidence needed.
