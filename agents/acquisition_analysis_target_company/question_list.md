# Acquisition Analysis (Target Company Side) — Question List

These questions collect the target company's decision context and available information before research. Users may answer "Not specified," "Unknown," or "Not available." Missing answers must not be converted into invented facts, preferences, or requirements.

| Question | Field ID | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| What is the deal background? | `deal_background` | Describe the approach, transaction history, current status, and decision to be supported. |
| What are the offer terms? | `offer_terms` | Provide consideration, value definition, payment mechanics, perimeter, conditions, and any amendments. |
| What are shareholders' priorities? | `shareholder_priorities` | Identify evidenced priorities concerning value, liquidity, timing, control, risk, and retained upside. Distinguish differences among holders. |
| What financials and forecasts are available? | `available_financials_and_forecasts` | Identify statements, forecasts, assumptions, periods, and their sources. Distinguish management forecasts from analyst scenarios. |
| Are there other offers or ongoing discussions? | `other_offers_and_discussions` | Identify actual offers or discussions and their status. Separate these from hypothetical alternatives. |
| Who are the key shareholders and decision-makers? | `shareholders_and_decision_makers` | Identify relevant holders, security classes, voting or approval rights, decision-makers, and known conflicts. |
| What approvals and deadlines apply? | `approvals_and_deadlines` | Identify known approvals, response dates, contractual deadlines, and timing constraints. |
| What are the negotiation priorities? | `negotiation_priorities` | Identify price, payment, liquidity, certainty, protections, or other priorities and explicit limits. |
| What documents are available, and what's in scope? | `available_documents_and_scope` | List available documents and locations, access restrictions, included work, and exclusions. Listing a document does not establish runtime access. |
